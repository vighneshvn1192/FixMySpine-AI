FAQ

Common Questions

Is FixMySpine AI a medical app?
No. FixMySpine AI is a wellness and ergonomic guidance app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any medical condition. It is designed for posture awareness, ergonomic education, and mobility guidance only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
Does the app upload my camera feed?
Never. All posture analysis happens 100% on your device using on-device AI. No video frames, images, or biometric data are ever transmitted or stored externally. Your camera feed is processed in real-time locally and immediately discarded.
Is it really free?
Yes, completely free. No subscriptions, no trials, no paywalls. The expert booking session is optional and the first consultation is free. The core posture tracking, breath coaching, and all AI features cost nothing.
What devices does it work on?
FixMySpine AI runs in any modern browser on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS. It works as a PWA (installable app) on all platforms, including Microsoft Store on Windows. A front-facing camera is required for posture analysis.
How accurate is the AI posture detection?
The AI tracks 33 body landmarks in real-time using on-device machine learning. Accuracy depends on lighting, camera position, and how much of your body is visible. The app displays a confidence score and only issues alerts above the 65% confidence threshold. Results are for wellness guidance — not clinical assessment.
I have a health condition — is it safe to use?
The app includes a health profile that activates additional safety reminders for conditions like high blood pressure, spinal conditions, and respiratory concerns. However, always consult your doctor before starting any new exercise or posture programme. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort.
Which languages are supported?
English, Hindi (हिन्दी), French (Français), German (Deutsch), Spanish (Español), Chinese (中文), and Arabic (العربية). All alerts, voice coaching, and AI responses are localised.
